## Publication boundary

The public site contains bibliographic metadata, links, citations and neutral descriptive organization only. Unpublished research notes, argument maps, corpus data, provenance-sensitive working records and private annotations remain outside this repository.

Treat each topic directory as a public derivative. When a source research corpus has a `CURRENT_STATE`, closure manifest, canonical bibliography manifest or evidence policy, that source-side authority controls scholarly status. Do not infer from the presence, absence or ordering of a citation on this site that a research claim is active, closed, verified, rejected or newly discovered.

## Update rule

Public bibliography updates should be additive or source-controlled. If a citation is corrected or removed, preserve enough bibliographic/provenance information to explain the change where it matters. Do not copy private working notes into this repository merely to keep the public page synchronized.

The site is intentionally lightweight: presentation files (`index.html`, `_includes/`, styles/configuration, sitemap/robots) support publication and do not constitute a second research backend.
