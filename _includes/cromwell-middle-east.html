Arabic, Hebrew, and Turkish receptions: serial translation, heroic biography, and comparative political images

  1. 1911–12

    Carlyle, Thomas. الأبطال وعبادة البطولة [al-Abṭāl wa-ʿibādat al-buṭūla / Heroes and Hero-Worship]. Translated into Arabic by محمد السباعي [Muhammad al-Sibāʿī] and published by Maktabat al-Bayān in connection with ʿAbd al-Raḥmān al-Barqūqī’s Cairo journal مجلة البيان. The journal introduced the translation in no. 2, printed material from Carlyle’s sixth lecture on Cromwell and Napoleon in no. 3, and in no. 8 (15 May 1912) published a substantial instalment headed “كرومويل - نابليون بونابرت” [“Cromwell – Napoleon Bonaparte”].

  2. 1943

    Carlyle, Thomas. Kahramanlar [Heroes]. Translated into Turkish by Reşat Nuri Güntekin, with a preface by Jean Izoulet. Istanbul: Semih Lütfi Kitabevi, [1943]. The final section, “Kral kahraman: Kromvel, Napolyon,” pp. 168–207, places Cromwell and Napoleon together as hero-kings. The Turkish Historical Society Library preserves a copy inscribed by Güntekin to historian and future prime minister Şemsettin Günaltay on 10 April 1946.

  3. 1947–48

    قلعجي، قدري [Qadri Qalaji]. كرومويل: بطل الثورة الإنكليزية [Cromwell: Hero of the English Revolution]. Beirut: دار العلم للملايين [Dār al-ʿIlm lil-Malāyīn], first published by 1947–48; later editions followed. Contemporary reception is documented by Hassan Saab’s article-review “كرومويل، بطل الثورة الإنكليزية” in the Beirut journal الأديب, no. 2 (1 February 1948). Later Arabic bibliographies record subsequent editions, including a 1956 edition.

  5. 2014

    Boyar, Ebru. “Türk-İngiliz İlişkilerinde Prestij Faktörü (1923–1938)” [“The Prestige Factor in Turkish-British Relations (1923–1938)”]. Belleten 78, no. 283 (2014): 1157–1194. Boyar traces foreign press constructions of Mustafa Kemal’s prestige, including a 1923 comparison with Cromwell, Napoleon, Washington, and Lincoln, while explicitly cautioning that this comparison reflected the foreign observer’s image of Mustafa Kemal rather than demonstrable Turkish self-description.
