Central and Eastern Europe: strong-man fiction, socialist translation, revolutionary biography, and local re-editing

  1. 1936

    Jelusich, Mirko. Oliver Cromwell. Translated by Maria Kreczowska [Maria Feldmanowa]. Warsaw: J. Przeworski, 1936 [bibliographical sources also record 1935 as the effective publication year]. Polish translation of Jelusich’s 1933 German-language historical novel Cromwell; surviving publicity frames “Cromwellism” as strikingly relevant to recent political events in Poland.
