Working bibliography

Microscope Slides

Preparation and mounting, circulation, collections, epistemic use, storage, deterioration, restoration, and conservation.

A working bibliography on microscope slides as material and epistemic objects. It combines historical manuals and commercial sources with scholarship on makers, circulation, scientific and medical use, collection histories, storage, deterioration, restoration, and conservation. Links lead to DOI records, open copies, institutional pages, or discovery records. Last revised 16 August 2026.
