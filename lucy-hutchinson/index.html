Working bibliography

Lucy Hutchinson

Manuscripts, editions, Civil War writing, religion, Lucretius and materialism, poetry, gender, reception, and selected documentary contexts.

This is a working research bibliography rather than a claim to exhaustiveness. It combines primary witnesses and editions with scholarship directly on Lucy Hutchinson and a smaller body of comparative work used to situate her writing. Entries have been cleaned from a larger research bibliography and are presented here without unpublished argument notes. Last revised 16 August 2026.
