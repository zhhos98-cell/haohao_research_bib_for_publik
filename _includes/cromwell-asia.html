I

South and Southeast Asia: Carlyle, vernacular exemplarity, educational canons, and political warning

  1. 1937

    রায়, প্রফুল্লচন্দ্র [Prafulla Chandra Ray]. আত্মচরিত [Ātmacarit / Autobiography]. Calcutta, 1937. In chapter 16, Ray draws on Carlyle’s Cromwell to exemplify disciplined labour and concentration after Dunbar and Worcester, then places Cromwell beside Mustafa Kemal Atatürk in a discussion of exceptional working capacity.

  2. 1941

    Ambedkar, B. R. Pakistan, or the Partition of India. Bombay: Thacker, 1941. The opening pages reapply Carlyle’s warning in Oliver Cromwell’s Letters and Speeches to the political circumstances of India.

  3. 1982

    Hart, Michael H. Seratus Tokoh yang Paling Berpengaruh dalam Sejarah. Translated into Indonesian by H. Mahbub Djunaidi. Jakarta: PT Dunia Pustaka Jaya, 1982. The Indonesian translation places Cromwell at no. 47 in Hart’s global ranking of historical influence; the work remained in Indonesian circulation through later editions.
